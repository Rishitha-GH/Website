What’s better than brainrot? — SLIME!

The most satisfying and brain-tickling activity: playing with slime. Slime can be soft, pillowy, wet, or crunchy — whatever suits your taste.

What is slime texture?

Slime texture is how slime feels when you stretch or press it. Different ingredients change how slime behaves and feels.

Slime Texture Rankings

Rank 1: Butter Slime

Butter slime is soft and smooth. It is easy to stretch and keeps its texture well.

Rank 2: Cloud Slime

Cloud slime is fluffy and light. It creates a drizzle effect when pulled apart.

Rank 3: Clear Slime

Clear slime is glossy and stretchy, but it can be harder to maintain.

Rank 4: Crunchy Slime

Crunchy slime has beads or charms inside. It makes noise but feels less smooth.

How the rankings were decided

The rankings are based on how the slime feels, how long it lasts, and how enjoyable it is to play with.
